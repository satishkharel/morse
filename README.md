# नेपाल ह्याम मोर्स ट्युटर · Nepal Ham Morse Tutor

नयाँ एमेच्योर रेडियो सञ्चालकहरूका लागि निःशुल्क मोर्स कोड (CW) अभ्यास कार्यक्रम। नेपाली र अङ्ग्रेजी दुवैमा।

## के-के छ · What's inside

- **एक-एक अक्षर अभ्यास** · Letter drill: one letter at a time; right answers in green, wrong in red
- **कोच पाठ** · Koch lessons (40 lessons, 41 characters), Farnsworth timing (15 / 5 WPM)
- कल साइन, ह्याम शब्द, अभ्यास QSO, आफ्नै पाठ · Callsigns, ham words, practice QSOs, free text
- **नमुना परीक्षा** · Mock exam in the Nepal Ministry format: 15 WPM characters, 5 WPM overall, five-letter words and 10 callsigns, 15 minutes, answer sheet unlocks 30 seconds after the test, pass mark 60%
- **पठाउने अभ्यास** · Sending practice with the Space bar, mouse or touch screen, with a timing report
